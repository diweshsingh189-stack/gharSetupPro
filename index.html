EXPERT RECOMMENDATIONS

Room-by-Room Tile Buying Guide

01 · LIVING ROOM

Living Room & Drawing Hall

Best Pick: 4x2 ft (600x1200 mm) or 800x1600 mm Glazed Vitrified (GVT / PGVT) High Gloss

  • ✓ Seamless look with minimal grout joints
  • ✓ High reflective index makes space look larger
  • ✓ Stain-resistant & easy mop cleaning
Approx. ₹55 - ₹120 / sq.ft
02 · BEDROOM

Master & Guest Bedrooms

Best Pick: Wooden Plank Vitrified (8x48") or Satin Matte Finish Vitrified (2x2 ft)

  • ✓ Warm, cozy natural wood texture without termite risk
  • ✓ Non-glare satin surface comfortable on eyes & feet
  • ✓ Highly durable with scratch resistant coating
Approx. ₹50 - ₹95 / sq.ft
03 · BATHROOM

Bathroom & Powder Rooms

Best Pick: Anti-Skid Matte Floor (1x1 ft) + 3D Highlighter Digital Wall (1x1.5 ft or 1x2 ft)

  • ✓ R10 Anti-Skid rating prevents slips when wet
  • ✓ Full height wall tiling prevents seepage & fungus
  • ✓ Darker floor + lighter wall gives stunning depth
Approx. ₹35 - ₹65 / sq.ft
04 · KITCHEN

Kitchen Floor & Backsplash

Best Pick: Stain-Resistant Matte Floor (2x2 ft) + High Gloss Ceramic Backsplash (300x600 mm)

  • ✓ Zero porosity prevents oil & turmeric stain absorption
  • ✓ Heat & steam resistant backsplash behind stove
  • ✓ Easy wiping with soapy water
Approx. ₹45 - ₹80 / sq.ft
05 · BALCONY & TERRACE

Balcony, Deck & Terrace

Best Pick: Cool Roof Reflective Tiles or Rustic Weather-Proof Matte Exterior Tiles

  • ✓ Cool Roof SRI > 100 reduces indoor temperature by 5°C
  • ✓ UV & frost resistant - does not fade in sunlight
  • ✓ Textured grip prevents wet monsoon skids
Approx. ₹48 - ₹85 / sq.ft
06 · PARKING & DRIVEWAY

Parking, Porch & Driveway

Best Pick: 12mm / 16mm Full Body Heavy Duty Vitrified (Johnson Endura / Somany)

  • ✓ Ultra-high load bearing capacity (can support SUVs & trucks)
  • ✓ Scratch-proof from tyres, pebbles, and stand friction
  • ✓ Full body material - color stays consistent even if chipped
Approx. ₹55 - ₹95 / sq.ft
