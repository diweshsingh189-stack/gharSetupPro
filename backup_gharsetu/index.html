CONSTRUCTION ROADMAP

From plot to home.

A general construction sequence. Actual execution should follow approved architectural and structural plans.

STAGE 01

Planning & Design

Finalize requirements, floor plan, architectural drawings, structural design and preliminary budget.

STAGE 02

Site Investigation

Check site levels, soil conditions, access, drainage and basic site requirements.

STAGE 03

Site Preparation

Clear the plot, establish levels and prepare the construction area.

STAGE 04

Foundation

Excavation, PCC, footing reinforcement, concrete and foundation-related work.

STAGE 05

Plinth

Complete plinth-related masonry, filling, damp protection and floor preparation.

STAGE 06

Columns & Beams

Construct the structural frame according to approved structural drawings.

STAGE 07

Brick Masonry

Construct external walls, internal walls and partitions.

STAGE 08

Slab / Roof

Complete shuttering, reinforcement, embedded services and concrete work.

STAGE 09

Electrical & Plumbing

Install conduits, wiring routes, water supply and drainage systems.

STAGE 10

Plaster & Waterproofing

Finish wall surfaces and waterproof bathrooms, roofs and wet areas.

STAGE 11

Flooring & Openings

Install flooring, tiles, doors, windows and hardware.

STAGE 12

Painting & Finishing

Complete putty, primer, paint, fixtures and final finishing.

STAGE 13

Testing & Inspection

Check electrical, plumbing, drainage, doors, windows and visible defects.

STAGE 14

Final Handover

Complete snag list, cleaning, documentation and final handover.
