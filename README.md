# 🏠 ghar.setup.pro
> **Smart Home Construction Planner & Architectural Design Estimator**

**ghar.setup.pro** is a complete, modern web platform designed to make home construction planning, budget estimation, material calculation, and timeline tracking seamless and transparent.

---

## ✨ Features
- 💰 **Detailed Itemized Cost Estimator**: Real-time project cost calculation based on built-up area and customizable material quantities.
- 🧱 **Material Library & Market Rates**: Rates and guidance for Cement, TMT Steel, Sand, Bricks, Tiles, Electrical, Plumbing, and more.
- 📐 **Measurement Calculators**:
  - Floor Area Calculator
  - Wall & Plaster Area Calculator
  - Concrete RCC Volume Calculator
  - Area Budget Cost Estimator
- 🏗️ **Phase-by-Phase Construction Roadmap**: 14 detailed structural stages and 20-day daily work schedule with material requirements.
- 🏠 **Trending 2026 Architectural House Gallery**: 12 curated modern home designs with full-resolution lightbox previews.
- ✅ **Interactive 27-Point Inspection Checklist**: Track construction milestones with auto-saved progress.
- 🖨️ **Project Reports & Data Export**: Instant PDF printable summary and JSON data backup/restore.
- 🌓 **Themes & UI**: Modern glassmorphism UI with Dark/Light mode and sound effects.

---

## 🚀 Getting Started

### Run Locally:
```bash
# Using npx serve
npx serve . -l 3000
```
Then open [http://localhost:3000](http://localhost:3000) in your browser.
